AeroTech 2030
A browser-based consultancy simulation.

Audience: MSc Engineering Business Management students.

Duration: 20–30 minutes.

Scenario: AeroTech Precision Ltd has used CATIA V5 for over 15 years. CATIA V5 is approaching sustaining support. The board must select a future CAD/CAM/CAE platform strategy.

Student Role: External consulting team.

Learning Outcomes:

Stakeholder analysis
Requirements capture
Engineering software evaluation
Change management
Risk mitigation
Strategic decision making
Desired Gameplay:

Board briefing
Stakeholder interviews
Evidence gathering
Recommendation
Board review
Year 1 / Year 3 / Year 5 outcomes
Desired Features:

10 stakeholders
Trust scores
Evidence categories
Limited question budget
Investigation coverage dashboard
Platform comparison
Dynamic board outcomes
Technology:

React
Vite
Azure Static Web Apps deployment
