# AeroTech 2030 Strategic Transformation Simulation

## Authoritative Redesign Brief

This document supersedes previous design guidance.

Audience: MSc Engineering Business Management students
Duration: 20–30 minutes ideal, 45 minutes maximum
Delivery: Individual or group-based consultancy exercise

---

# Core Reframe

The simulation is NOT about choosing CAD software.

The simulation IS about advising the Board of AeroTech Precision Ltd on a strategic technology transformation decision under uncertainty.

Software selection is a consequence of strategic decision-making, not the primary objective.

Students should feel they are:

- consultants
- strategy analysts
- transformation advisors
- board advisors

They should not feel they are:

- collecting clues
- maximising trust scores
- completing dialogue trees

---

# Learning Outcomes

Students should demonstrate:

- Stakeholder analysis
- Technology strategy evaluation
- Requirements elicitation
- Risk identification
- Assumption testing
- Change management awareness
- Evidence-based recommendation development

---

# Simulation Structure

## Phase 1: Executive Briefing (3–5 Minutes)

Students receive:

### CEO Memorandum

AeroTech Precision Ltd has relied upon CATIA V5 for more than fifteen years.

Changes to product lifecycle support arrangements mean the Board must now determine the organisation's future engineering technology strategy.

Any recommendation must balance:

- competitiveness
- implementation risk
- operational continuity
- workforce capability
- customer expectations
- financial performance

### Organisation Overview

Revenue: ~£150M

Employees: ~450

Engineering Users: ~220

Industry: Aerospace Components

Customers:
- Airbus
- Rolls-Royce
- BAE Systems

Current Environment:
- CATIA V5
- Legacy CAM workflows
- Existing simulation integrations

Board Deadline:
6 months

---

## Phase 2: Investigation (15–20 Minutes)

Students receive:

12 Investigation Actions

Examples:

- Interview stakeholder
- Review internal report
- Review customer feedback
- Review competitor activity
- Review risk assessment

Students CANNOT investigate everything.

The challenge is deciding what information matters.

---

# Stakeholder Design

Every stakeholder contains:

## Public Position

What they say openly.

## Underlying Concern

What really drives their behaviour.

## Blind Spot

What they consistently underestimate.

## Influence

Low / Medium / High.

## Evidence

Information they can reveal.

## Challenge Question

A question they ask the student team.

Students should occasionally be forced to answer challenging questions.

---

# Evidence Framework

Classifications:

## Strategic Driver

Reason change may be required.

## Customer Requirement

Expectation emerging from customers.

## Constraint

Factor limiting available options.

## Risk

Threat to success.

## Workforce Issue

Skills, recruitment or capability concern.

## Opportunity

Potential value creation.

## Assumption

Belief requiring validation.

Track:

- Cost

- Customer Requirements
- Future Readiness
- Training

- Supply Chain

Status:

Green = Investigated
Amber = Partial
Red = Neglected

---

# Contradiction System

High-quality consulting requires dealing with contradictory evidence.

Examples:

Finance:
"Major investment introduces significant risk."

Airbus:
"Failure to invest introduces significant strategic risk."

Both may be correct.

Students must navigate trade-offs.

---

# Strategic Pathways

## Option A
Retain CATIA V5 Short-Term

Benefit:
Low disruption.

Risk:
Capability stagnation.

---

## Option B
Adopt 3DEXPERIENCE

Benefit:
Continuity within Dassault ecosystem.

Risk:
Large transformation programme.

---

## Option C
Transform Around Siemens NX

Benefit:
Integrated engineering and manufacturing capability.

Risk:
Major migration effort.

---

## Option D
Phased Hybrid Strategy

Benefit:
Reduced implementation shock.

Risk:
Extended transition period.

# Recommendation Requirements

Students must submit:

1. Recommended strategy
2. Five supporting evidence items
3. Three major risks
4. Two assumptions
5. Three mitigations

# Success Criteria

Students should finish discussing:

- What evidence mattered most?
- What did we miss?
- Which assumptions were weak?
- Which stakeholder perspectives conflicted?
- Would another recommendation also have been valid?

The simulation rewards:

QUALITY OF INVESTIGATION

not

SELECTION OF A PARTICULAR SOFTWARE PLATFORM
